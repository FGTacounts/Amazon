UNIT PROFIT (owner, 24 Oct: never sell at a loss or at break-even — foresee Amazon's commission and all expenses)
For any SKU, per unit at a given price P (AED, VAT-inclusive):
  net = P ÷ 1.05 (remove 5% VAT) − Amazon referral fee − closing/variable fee − fulfilment cost (FBA fulfilment fee, or MFN delivery/packing cost) − storage share (monthly FBA storage fee ÷ units sold per month) − returns allowance (2% of P) − ad cost per unit (that SKU's last-30-day ad spend ÷ its units sold; for a new SKU use the target ACoS × P) − landed cost.
  margin % = net ÷ landed cost. Minimum 30% (owner's rule; target 40%). Break-even ACoS = (net before ad cost) ÷ P.
Take the fees from Seller Central's fee preview / Revenue Calculator for that ASIN, not guesses. If landed cost or fees are unknown, treat the SKU as "cost unknown": no price changes and no deals on it (raising a price to match Amazon's vendor price is always allowed).
